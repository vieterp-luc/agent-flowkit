## Production config (Google Flow — full pipeline)
- Pipeline: `/fk-create-project` → `/fk-gen-refs` → `/fk-gen-images` → `/fk-gen-videos` → `/fk-gen-narrator` → `/fk-concat-fit-narrator` → `/fk-gen-caption`.
- Project: VERTICAL · material `realistic` · 7 scene · narrator VN 20–22 từ · `Anh_Khoi_TTS` 0.95 (full 3-câu ref_text).
- **Images (`/fk-gen-images`):** Wave 1 = ROOT (`GENERATE_IMAGE`), Wave 2 = S2 CONTINUATION (`EDIT_IMAGE`) sau khi Wave 1 xong. Prompt EN macro/vertical/no-text/safe-words.
- **Videos (`/fk-gen-videos`) — đốt credits nhất, bắt buộc 3 guardrails:**
  1. Pre-flight `curl /api/flow/credits` ≥ 30 (mỗi scene ~5–15 credits; 7 scene ⇒ cần ~50–100).
  2. Probe-first: submit 1 scene → verify COMPLETED → mới submit 6 scene còn lại.
  3. Halt-on-quota-error: gặp `QUOTA_REACHED` / `no operations` / `MODEL_ACCESS_DENIED` ⇒ PATCH tất cả PENDING/PROCESSING về FAILED retry_count=5 trước khi worker auto-retry 5×.
- **Concat (`/fk-concat-fit-narrator`):** xfade 0.5s cho chain S1↔S2; buffer 0.5s; TTS 1.5× / SFX 0.3×; 720×1280 @24fps. Logo 90px. Caption FB+TikTok.
- ⚠️ Safe-words bắt buộc cho #1 (screwworm) & #7 (chigoe flea): dùng *burrow into skin / larvae emerging / swelling / tissue* thay vì *eat flesh / wound / infest* trong cả image + video prompt để né Flow filter (`UNSAFE_GENERATION`).
